# Paid Media & Marketing Operations Portfolio

I run paid search, paid social and the measurement behind them for **high-ticket, high-consideration services**, where buyers compare several firms before they call and success is judged on lead quality, not clicks.

This repository shows *how* I work: the strategy, the decisions, the things that went wrong, and the small tools I built to catch them. Client details are anonymised and figures are described in relative terms on purpose.

## What I do

- **Google Ads:** intent-based account structure, keyword research, negative-keyword hygiene, bidding decisions on low-conversion-volume accounts, ad copy testing
- **Meta Ads:** diagnosis before rebuild, positioning, fair creative testing, Conversions API
- **Measurement and attribution:** tag manager, GA4, pixel and consent fixes, call tracking, CRM-to-ad-platform feedback loops
- **Operations:** automated daily and weekly checks, reporting workbooks, incident write-ups
- **SEO and landing pages:** keyword mapping, cannibalisation fixes, single-audience conversion pages
- **Brand and content systems:** positioning, content calendars, brand guides, creative direction

## A few principles I work by

- **Measurement first.** On a low-volume account, a tracking fault looks exactly like a performance problem.
- **Cross-check platform numbers against the CRM.** Platforms report what they were told.
- **Test fairly or don't test.** Equal delivery, one variable, a read date set in advance.
- **A negative keyword is a permanent decision.** Check for lost leads before you block.
- **Say what isn't solved.** Honest limits make the rest of the report believable.
